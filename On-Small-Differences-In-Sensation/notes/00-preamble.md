On Small Differences in Sensation — example notes

Started 7 August 2026. The paper is interactive as of 7 August: build.sh now
assembles ../shared/lib.js, ../shared/scaffold.js and src/10_examples.js.

Because src/02_article.html is generated by tools/render.py, no trigger or
container is typed into it — they would be overwritten. tools/examples.py runs
after render.py and puts them in from the `anchor:` and `container:` of each
notes entry. So writing an example means: register it in src/10_examples.js, and
put `container:` into its notes entry beside the `anchor:` that is already
there. An entry with an anchor but no container is a spec and is skipped.

Entries here follow _status/CONVENTIONS.md. Add one by selecting a passage on
the page with the review server running and clicking "+ Add example" — that
writes the file, with the passage as its anchor.

The nine tables are the obvious first candidates: they are already rendered as
data rather than as pictures of data.
