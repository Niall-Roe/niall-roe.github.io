Peirce's dictionary entries

About these entriesPeirce wrote some 6,158 dictionary entries: 5,973 for the Century Dictionary (1889–91) and 185 for Baldwin's Dictionary of Philosophy and Psychology (1901–02), 30 of them with a co-author. Kenneth Laine Ketner and Elize Bisanz have gone through and listed which definitions are Peirce's. This site uses their lists and publicly available copies of the dictionaries to create a searchable collection of all his definitions. The text shown is a machine reading of the scanned pages of these dictionaries and may contain errors.
