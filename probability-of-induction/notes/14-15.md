#### the stutter

Seven of the live figures in Peirce's sentence — the composition, the truth, the
denominator, the two counts and the two proportions — were each read off a fresh
call to the same routine, and that routine builds and sorts a table with one row
per possible number of white balls. So a single nudge of the proportion slider
rebuilt and re-sorted the same table seven times over; at a sample of 500 that is
3,500 rows of work per frame, which is what you were feeling.

The table now depends only on the two sliders, so the last answer is kept and the
other six calls read it. Measured on the built page at the worst setting, sample
size 500: 3.5 ms per drag step, against a 16 ms frame. Nothing about what is
drawn or computed has changed — the same numbers, worked once instead of seven
times.

The crazy proportions are untouched.
