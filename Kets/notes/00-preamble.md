Kets — example notes. Started 13 August 2026.

Peirce's text (CP 1.208–211, the Naucratis balance-weights passage from the
Minute Logic) is in place. First build pass 13 August 2026: fifteen examples
built and awaiting approval, numbered 1–16 in reading order down the page
(5 is reserved for the parked "alternative view" entry). New entries are
created from the review overlay by selecting the passage to anchor on.

# The data

Petrie's determinations live in `Kets/data/` — see `data/README.md` for full
provenance and the verification record:

  - `naukratis1888-kat.csv` — the 158-ket table from Naukratis I, transcribed
    fresh from the scan and verified row by row (13 Aug 2026);
  - `kets-peirce.csv` — the tenth-of-a-grain subset Peirce describes;
  - the much larger 1926 register (rough, unverified) for later.

Peirce's own numbers: 158 weights, "144" used, range 136.8–151.3 grs, no gap
wider than a third of a grain, five standards at 139.2 / 142.2 / 144.7 /
146.95 / 149.7 grs carrying 36 / 25 / 26 / 23 / 34 weights, probable error of
a single weight five-eighths of a grain. Caution for examples: the printed
table gives 142 tenth-precision kets, not 144, and the no-gap claim has three
0.4–0.5 grain exceptions — see the README. An example could make a virtue of
this ("rough-and-ready theory" indeed).

# House style

Same as the other papers: anchor triggers on the sentence stating the result;
drive Peirce's own figures in his own sentences; sliders wherever the quantity
is ordered; the standards table in the article is the natural host for live
numbers, as the granary table is in the Probability of Induction.
