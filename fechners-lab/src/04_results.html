What a threshold is used for

A single threshold is a small fact about one weight. Two further steps turn it into a scale.

Step 1

The same subject at other standards

Nothing so far says he behaves the same way at 25 g or 400 g. Measure the difference threshold again at each, by the first method.

The difference threshold in grams, and the same thresholds as a fraction of their standard.

Weber's law The thresholds in grams range widely. As a fraction of the standard they stay put. This is a finding about the subject, not a definition: he could have come out otherwise.

Step 2

Counting thresholds

Fechner's step. Call one difference threshold one unit of sensation, the same size wherever it falls. Start at the absolute threshold and climb, one unit at a time.

Fechner's law Nobody assumed a logarithm; the counting produced one. What fixed the bottom of the scale was the absolute threshold. Move that and every number on the scale moves with it.
